What it opens

Images
PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP, BMP, TIFF, HEIC
Documents
SVG, PDF, Word, Excel, Jupyter notebooks
Text
Markdown, HTML, plain text and source, JSON and JSONL, CSV
3D
STL and 3MF meshes, with their parts and colors

In your terminal

gloss is a single Go binary. Pictures are drawn with Kitty graphics where the terminal has them, and with glyphs everywhere else. Drop files on it, browse folders, and export what you see.

$ go install github.com/NimbleMarkets/gloss/cmd/gloss@latest

In your browser

The same gloss, compiled to WebAssembly, for your own files: drop them, open them with the browser's picker, and find them again next time. They are read and kept in your browser, and nowhere else.
